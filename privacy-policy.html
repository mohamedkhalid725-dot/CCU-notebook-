CardioVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

CardioVault is a clinical notebook designed for cardiology and critical care practice.

Information we process

The app may process account information supplied by your authentication provider, such as an account identifier, email address, and display name. It also processes clinical information that you choose to enter, including patient records, observations, laboratory results, medications, procedures, and clinical notes.

How information is used

Information is used to provide authentication, patient-record features, synchronization, reporting, and security functions requested by the user.

Local storage and cloud synchronization

CardioVault is designed for local-first use. Clinical data can be stored locally on the device. If cloud synchronization is enabled, relevant application data is synchronized through Firebase services associated with the CardioVault project.

Third-party services

CardioVault uses Google/Firebase services for authentication and optional cloud synchronization. Those services may process information as necessary to provide the requested functionality and according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal or clinical information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers when necessary to provide features that you request.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application data, but no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until it is removed or the application data is cleared. Cloud-synchronized data may remain according to the applicable account and service processes. For deletion assistance, contact the developer through the support channel associated with the CardioVault listing.

Children

CardioVault is intended for professional clinical use and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, use the developer support/contact channel associated with the CardioVault application listing.
